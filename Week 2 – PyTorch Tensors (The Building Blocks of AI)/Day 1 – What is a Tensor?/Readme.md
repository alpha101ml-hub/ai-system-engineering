- GPUs are essential for rapidly training deep learning models,
- while CPUs manage data preprocessing and lighter inference tasks

### What does dtype mean in PyTorch
- In PyTorch, dtype stands for "data type" and represents the underlying numerical
- format of the data stored within a torch.Tensor
